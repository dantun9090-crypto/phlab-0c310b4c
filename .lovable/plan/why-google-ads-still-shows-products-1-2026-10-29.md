# Why Google Ads still shows "Products (1)"

## What the screenshot means
- **Products (1):** Google still remembers the product you moved to the archive. Merchant Center reads the product list only about once a day. Google Ads then needs another 1–3 days to clear the warning, so it can stay up after the product is gone.
- **Asset group limited by policy:** a different issue. One headline, image or the landing page touches Google's health policy. This is normal for peptides and lets the ad show to fewer people. It does not block the ad.
- **Ad strength poor:** the campaign has too few headlines, descriptions or images. It still runs, but it reaches fewer people.

## Plan (check only, nothing in Google Ads changes)
1. Confirm the archived product is gone from both product files (the main one and the free one).
2. Look up the "Products (1)" item in Google Ads and confirm it is the archived product, not a different one.
3. Find which headline, image or link set off "limited by policy", and why.
4. List what is missing for better Ad strength.
5. Report back in plain language. Any fix in Google Ads waits for your approval.

## What you can do now
In Merchant Center, open Products → Feeds, pick the file and click **Fetch now**. The warning should clear within 1–3 days.
